Concept matching
----------------
The build script selects an illustration by the chapter title (for example, AI
chapters use a machine-learning workflow, database chapters use a database
architecture diagram, and network chapters use a topology diagram). A chapter
that already contains a diagram or photograph keeps its existing illustration.

Human-photo policy
------------------
The merged-book generator adds genuine photographs of people to people-centred
chapters (for example, data entry, training, employability and computer learning)
when the chapter does not already contain a recognizable human photograph.
Technical chapters keep concept-matched diagrams/screenshots and receive a human
photograph only where the chapter topic makes it useful. Images are fetched from
Wikimedia Commons during the build; no synthetic/AI-generated pictures are used.
